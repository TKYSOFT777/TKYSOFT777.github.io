Privacy Policy

Bar Craft · Last updated: November 15, 2026

Bar Craft does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

Your progress (scores, stars, settings and language) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.

The Support and Privacy Policy links in Settings open this website in your browser.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
