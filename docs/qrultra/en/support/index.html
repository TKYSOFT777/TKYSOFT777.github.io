FAQ

The camera view is black and nothing scans.

Camera access may be turned off. Go to Settings › QRultra › Camera on your iPhone and turn it on.

Small codes or dark places are hard to scan.

Tap “2×” at the bottom to zoom, or turn on the light at the top left. If you are too close, the camera may not focus, so try moving back a little.

Do I need to center the code on the screen?

No. Any code visible on the screen is scanned. If several codes are visible, the one closest to the center is scanned.

How do I scan the same code again after closing the result?

Keep the code in view and it is read again about one second after you close the result. To scan it again right away, tap the button at the bottom center.

How do I scan a QR code in a screenshot?

Tap the photo button at the top right of the scan screen and pick the image. On iPad, you can also drag and drop an image.

Can I change the browser used to open links?

Links open in your default browser. To change it, go to Settings › Apps › Default Apps › Browser App on your iPhone.

Can links open without showing the result?

Turn on “Open links immediately” in Settings. Links then open in the browser as soon as they are scanned.

How do I change the app language?

The app follows your device language. To change it for QRultra only, go to Settings › QRultra › Language on your iPhone.

Can I move my history to another device?

No. To protect your privacy, history is stored only on your device.

I don’t want to keep history.

Turn off “Save history” in Settings. To delete saved history, tap “Clear All History”.

How do I open the app from Control Center?

Edit Control Center and add QRultra. You can also assign it to the Action button or the Lock Screen.
