Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 18, 2026 · Developer: TKYSOFT

QRultra (“the app”) does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. This policy explains how the app uses features and data on your device.

1. Information we collect

The app sends no data to the developer or to any third-party server. It uses no accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party crash reporting tools.

2. Device features

FeatureWhen it is usedHow it is handled
CameraScanning QR codesVideo is analyzed on the device in real time and is never saved or sent. The cut-out image of a scanned code is shown only while its result is open and is not saved.
PhotosScanning an image you pick with “Scan from Photo”Only the selected image is analyzed on the device; it is never saved or sent.
ContactsWhen you tap “Add to Contacts” on a scanned contactA contact is added only when you choose to; existing contacts are never read.
CalendarWhen you tap “Add to Calendar” on a scanned eventAn event is added only when you choose to; existing events are never read.
Wi-FiWhen you tap “Join This Network”The network details are passed to iOS only and never sent to the developer.

3. Scan history

Scanned content and timestamps are stored as history on your device only. History is not synced through iCloud or any other service. You can turn history off or clear it in Settings. Deleting the app also deletes the history.

4. External websites

Opening a scanned URL shows the page in your default browser. Those websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page. If the app ever begins collecting data, you will be notified in the app beforehand.
